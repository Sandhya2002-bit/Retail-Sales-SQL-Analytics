# Retail Sales Business Insights

## Key Findings

### Regional Performance
- West region generated the highest sales revenue.
- South region showed lower profitability compared to other regions.

### Product Analysis
- Office Supplies generated the highest overall profit among all product categories.
- Technology products also performed positively and contributed significantly to profitability.
- Furniture category recorded a negative total profit, indicating potential issues such as high discounts, shipping costs, or low-margin products.
- The business may need to review pricing and discount strategies for Furniture products to improve profitability.

### Customer Insights
- Top customers contributed a significant percentage of total revenue.

### Sales Trends
- Sales increased during year-end months.
- Monthly sales trends showed seasonal purchasing behavior.

### Discount Impact
- Higher discounts often reduced profitability.
- Certain categories were more sensitive to discounting strategies.
